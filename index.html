Where the Ganga
Carves Through Stone

Nestled at the foothills of the Garhwal Himalayas, Rishikesh is India's undisputed rafting capital. Our grade II–IV rapids wind through ancient river canyons, past Shiva temples and dense sal forests — offering thrills that range from a gentle float to heart-pounding white water.

We pair every rafting journey with riverside camping, bonfire evenings, and the raw beauty of the Himalayan night sky. This isn't a package — it's a passage.

Our Routes

Rapids & Routes

Three legendary stretches. Choose your current.

Grade II

Shivpuri Float

Perfect for first-timers. Gentle currents, scenic canyon walls, and the famous "Roller Coaster" rapid. A 9 km stretch ideal for families.

  • 9 km river stretch
  • ~2 hours rafting
  • Swimming spots included
From ₹1,200 9 km
Grade III

Brahmapuri Rush

The classic Rishikesh experience. Narrow gorges, powerful rapids like "Club House" and "Golf Course," with enough calm stretches for cliff jumping.

  • 16 km river stretch
  • ~3.5 hours rafting
  • Cliff jumping en route
From ₹2,500 16 km
Grade IV

Marine Drive

For seasoned adventurers. Technical rapids, massive drops, and the ultimate adrenaline rush. Requires prior rafting experience. The river's wildest face.

  • 26 km river stretch
  • ~5 hours rafting
  • Technical rapids only
From ₹3,800 26 km
